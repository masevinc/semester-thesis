Per-case definitions (for case i):

MSEi: mean squared error on the valid grid points.
MSEi = (1/Ni) ∑j (vtuij − npzij)^2
RMSEi: root mean squared error.
RMSEi = sqrt(MSEi)
Rel_MSEi: relative MSE using NPZ “energy” as reference.
Rel_MSEi = MSEi / [(1/Ni) ∑j (npzij)^2]
RMSE_pct_i: RMSE as a percent of the NPZ dynamic range for that case.
RMSE_pct_i = 100 × RMSEi / (max(npzi) − min(npzi))
All sums/means are over valid grid points (finite after interpolation and masking).

Macro (unweighted, per-case average)

macro_mse_mean: average of MSEi across cases.
macro_mse_mean = mean_i(MSEi)
macro_mse_median: median of MSEi across cases.
macro_mse_median = median_i(MSEi)
macro_mse_std: standard deviation of MSEi across cases.
macro_mse_std = std_i(MSEi)
macro_rel_mse_mean: average of Rel_MSEi across cases.
macro_rel_mse_mean = mean_i(Rel_MSEi)
macro_rmse_pct_mean: average of RMSE_pct_i across cases.
macro_rmse_pct_mean = mean_i(RMSE_pct_i)

Micro (weighted by number of grid points, across all cases) Let SSEi = ∑j (vtuij − npzij)^2, ENERGi = ∑j (npzij)^2, Ni = # valid points in case i. Then:

micro_mse: global MSE over all points.
micro_mse = (∑i SSEi) / (∑i Ni)
micro_rmse: sqrt of micro_mse.
micro_rmse = sqrt(micro_mse)
micro_rel_mse: global relative MSE over all points.
micro_rel_mse = (∑i SSEi) / (∑i ENERGi)
How to interpret

Macro = treats each case equally (good for “per-case” fairness). Median is robust to outliers; std shows spread.
Micro = treats every grid point equally (good for “overall pixel/point” performance). If cases have different sizes, larger ones weigh more.
It’s normal that macro_mse_mean ≈ micro_mse only when Ni are similar across cases. In your snippet they match, suggesting similar Ni or cancellation.
macro_rmse_pct_mean (4.703) is the average per-case RMSE as a percent of each case’s NPZ range—unitless, intuitive for relative scale.
